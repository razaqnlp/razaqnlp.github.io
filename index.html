Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I work on Pashto?

Pashto has millions of speakers, but compared with major languages, its NLP ecosystem has fewer datasets, benchmarks, and language-specific resources. My interest is in helping close that resource and technology gap.

Why start with Pashto if my broader goal is low-resource NLP?

Starting with a language I understand well allows me to investigate NLP problems more deeply and critically. I can recognize linguistic patterns, evaluate generated or annotated data, and identify problems that might otherwise be difficult to notice. This makes Pashto a useful starting point for developing methods and research questions that can later be explored across other low-resource languages.

Why Romanized Pashto?

Digital communication does not always follow formal writing conventions. Romanized Pashto can contain inconsistent spellings, multiple transliteration patterns, and Urdu/English code-mixing. I became interested in understanding how NLP models handle this naturally occurring language.

What is my main research focus?

My broader focus is multilingual and low-resource NLP, with particular interest in Pashto, Romanized and code-mixed language, language resources, and the behavior of multilingual and LLM systems.

Am I the first to work on this?

I do not claim to be a pioneer. My goal is to contribute to an emerging and relatively underexplored area. Where a specific contribution is genuinely novel, I establish that through a literature review and appropriate research claims.

Why am I interested in NLP research?

I am interested in the gap between the rapid development of language AI and the many languages and language varieties that receive comparatively little attention. Working with Pashto made that gap particularly tangible to me.

What do I actually want to understand?

Not only whether a model works, but why it works or fails. I am particularly interested in data quality, dataset composition, tokenization, representation, multilingual transfer, and generalization.

What matters most to me in research?

Building useful resources is important, but I care equally about understanding the underlying reasons for model behavior, producing reproducible research, and making language technology more inclusive.
